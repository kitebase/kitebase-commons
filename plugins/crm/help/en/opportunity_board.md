**A day on the board.** Each card is a negotiation, each column a stage. Start
from the colour on the cards: red is a step overdue, orange is due today, green
is planned; a card with no colour has nothing planned, and a negotiation with
nothing planned is one going cold.

- **Move a card** to another column when the negotiation moves on. A move tried
  and corrected a moment later leaves one line in its story, not two.
- **Plan the next step** before leaving a card: a call, a meeting, a reminder.
  It is what the card shows until it is done.
- **Close it** with Won or Lost, never by dragging. Lost asks why: the reasons
  are what tells, later, where negotiations stop. A closed card leaves the
  board and stays in the list of opportunities.
- **Click a card** to see its story beside the board, and write a call or a note
  there without opening it. Double-click it to open the whole negotiation.

**More than one kind of negotiation.** If your business has more than one
pipeline (new customers, renewals), pick it above the board: the columns are
its stages, and a new card is born in the first of them.

**Whose cards.** The board opens on your own open negotiations. To see a
colleague's, or everyone's, change or remove the rules of the filter: they are
there to be changed.
