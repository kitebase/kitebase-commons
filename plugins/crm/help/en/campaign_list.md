**How a campaign works.** A campaign is a list of people to contact and follow
up, and at the end a measure of what it brought.

1. **Create the campaign**, with a name and, if you like, its dates.
2. **Contact the partners.** In a list of partners tick the ones to contact and
   use *Contact for a campaign*: each one gets a contact to do, in the campaign
   and in its own history.
3. **Follow up.** Here, click the campaign: below are its contacts, the ones
   still to do first. When a contact goes well, *Create opportunity* turns it
   into a negotiation with that partner and this campaign, and opens it to be
   completed. When the partner is not interested, mark the contact done, with a
   note if it helps.
4. **Read what it brought.** The list counts the partners contacted, the
   contacts still to handle, the opportunities born from the campaign, the won
   ones and their value. They change as you work, without reloading.

A partner contacted for a campaign can still become an opportunity later, from
the partner's list: choose the campaign in the form, if it is the one that
brought them.
